            <h1>Language Use On Reddit</h1>
        </div>
        
        <div class="item">
            <h3>What is Reddit?</h3>
            <p>
                Reddit is a website that hosts a number of smaller online forums, called subreddits, where users, referred to as “redditors,” can tell stories, ask questions, and respond to others in comments. While redditors have the ability to share photos and videos, interaction is primarily text-based. Some popular subreddits include “IAmA” where users describe a unique or unusual aspect of their identity to invite questions from other redditors about their experiences, “Am I the Asshole,” where users can describe a situation they’ve experienced to determine if they were in the wrong, and “Am I Overreacting.” 
            </p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="item">
            <h3>Why Reddit?</h3>
            <p>
                Reddit provides a particularly interesting opportunity for the study of English use in large part because of its scale. While over 40% of Reddit users are from the United States, the website is used on all six populated continents, allowing for a large variation of influences on language use. Reddit also presents an opportunity for users to encounter cultural barriers in addition to those posed by different language backgrounds. Additionally, Reddit is easily accessible, particularly due to their “default open” policy, which dictates that “most of Reddit’s platform is public and accessible to everyone, even without an account” (Public Content Policy). This is done so that Reddit can remain “a place where all people can find community, belonging, and empowerment” (Public Content Policy), and it is this effort to find community on a large and diverse platform that makes the interactions of redditors linguistically interesting. 
            </p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="item">
            <h3>Research Questions</h3>
            <ol>
                <li>What vocabulary or specialized terms are used within this setting?</li>
                <li>In what ways can an online group alter or develop new language to convey tone?</li>
                <li>How might language use encourage or discourage participation?</li>
            </ol>
        </div>
        
        <div class="thesis">
            <p>
                While linguistic patterns of reddit users do create some boundaries to clear communication, particularly due to cultural references and widespread use of specialized language, the platform also presents an opportunity for a multitude of voices to be heard, regardless of what may be conceived as “proper” English. 
            </p>
