Specialized Language

Specialized language is used commonly across Reddit, although the extent of its use may vary depending on which subreddit one is using. The use of specialized language creates barriers to entry for those who may not know the terminology, particularly because some terms, such as “karma” already have English meanings that differ from the ways in which they are used on Reddit. Some of the most commonly used terms are defined below.

Upvote / Downvote

Up and down arrows appear next to each post and comment. They function as a voting system, allowing users to “determine what is displayed and surfaced in communities and conversations.” If users “like something [or] think it contributes to the conversation,” they can upvote it, and it will appear more prominently. The opposite is true for downvotes.

Karma

Karma is “a reflection of the upvotes and downvotes you receive on posts and comments you’ve made.” Upvotes and karma don’t have a 1 to 1 relationship, but the more upvotes someone gets, the more karma they’ll receive. Some communities require a certain amount of karma before a user can make a post.

Mod / Mods

Mods, short for moderators, are “redditors who volunteer their time to help create, guide, and nurture Reddit’s many communities.” Their primary responsibilities include removing posts or comments that don’t fit with the theme of a community, banning users who break community rules, and reviewing reports submitted by users.

Sub / Subs

Sub is short for “subreddit,” which refers to the individual communities within reddit.
