Analysis and Conclusion

Taken as a whole, it is easy to see all of the ways in which Reddit can create barriers between those who are already aware of the terminology used and those who are not. However, the tendency towards abbreviation is not a new phenomenon, as evidenced by Wric Partridge’s publication of a dictionary of abbreviations in 1942, which included terms such as “mth,” meaning month, “agn,” meaning again, and “gd,” meaning good (Kadir, Maros, and Hamid, 2). Additionally, while the official “reddiquette” states that users should “use proper grammar and spelling” because “intelligent discourse requires a standard system of communication” (Reddiquette), the widespread use of emojis, simplified language, and images or gifs proves that this is not a requirement that is often, if ever, enforced. As such, it can be concluded that while there are some linguistic patterns on Reddit that could create boundaries or in-group out-group separation, Reddit presents a unique opportunity for cross-cultural and cross-lingual communication.

It should, however, be noted that the rules upholding “proper” English are built in to Reddit at multiple points, which further upholds the common belief of English as a “default” language, both in terms of online and in person communication, placing English, and those who speak it, in a position of global power and privilege.
